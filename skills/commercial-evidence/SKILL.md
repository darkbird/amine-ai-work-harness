# Commercial Evidence

## Purpose

Convert verified professional evidence into reusable commercial communication.

## Required source

Prefer:
- proof-of-work achievement records
- weekly proof-of-work reports
- evidence referenced by those records

Do not mine raw code first if verified proof already exists.

## Claim ladder

1. Verified fact
2. Demonstrated capability
3. Potential business value
4. Measured outcome

Level 4 requires explicit evidence. Never promote potential value into a measured result.

## Outputs

Maintain under `_project-context/commercial/`:

- portfolio.md
- case-study-candidates.md
- cv-bullets.md
- proposal-evidence.md
- client-report-material.md
- content-ideas.md

## Rule

Every significant commercial claim must be traceable to a proof-of-work record or its evidence.

Never publish, send, or post externally without explicit instruction.
