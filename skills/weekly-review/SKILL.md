# Weekly Review

## Purpose

Create a decision-useful weekly review, not a diary.

## Inputs

Prefer per active workspace:
- STATE.md
- recent worklogs
- BACKLOG.md
- DECISIONS.md
- latest project audit
- current week's proof-of-work

## Questions

1. What materially moved forward?
2. What was completed and verified?
3. What remains open?
4. What is blocked?
5. Which projects are drifting or stagnating?
6. What proof-of-work was captured?
7. What useful work lacks evidence?
8. What carries into next week?
9. What can be deferred or archived?
10. Which decisions require human input?

## Output

```markdown
# Weekly Review — YYYY-WXX

## Executive view
## Material progress
## Completed and verified
## Active blockers
## Open loops
## Project drift / risks
## Proof of work captured
## Missing evidence
## Decisions needed
## Carry-over
## Next-week focus
```

Do not invent hours worked, score personal performance, or equate commit count with productivity.
